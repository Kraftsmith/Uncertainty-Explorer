## **Functional Requirements: Assessment Results Save & Email**

### **1. Save Assessment Results**

SAR-1 When a user completes the assessment and clicks **Send Summary to Email**, the system must save all answers and calculated scores for both the Stacey Matrix and Cynefin Framework.
SAR-2 The saved results must include:
  SAR-2.1 The user's email address (where the summary will be sent)
  SAR-2.2 All individual question responses for both frameworks
  SAR-2.3 Calculated summary scores and zone/domain classifications
  SAR-2.4 The date and time the assessment was submitted
SAR-3 The user must not need to take any extra steps to save their results; saving happens automatically upon submission.

### **2. Email Assessment Summary**

EAS-4 The email must also contain:
  EAS-4.1 Visual charts representing the assessment results (Stacey Matrix and Cynefin Framework)
  EAS-4.2 An overview of the assessment
  EAS-4.3 Delivery Framework section with recommended practices
  EAS-4.4 Recommended Roles section based on the assessment

EAS-1 After submitting the assessment, the user must receive an email at the address they provided.
EAS-2 The email must contain:
  EAS-2.1 A summary of their Stacey Matrix results, including scores and zone
  EAS-2.2 A summary of their Cynefin Framework results, including scores and domain
  EAS-2.3 Any key recommendations or next steps based on their results
EAS-3 The email must be sent automatically, without requiring further user action.

### **3. User Feedback and Confirmation**

UFC-1 After clicking **Send Summary to Email**, the user must see a clear confirmation message on the screen, such as:
  UFC-1.1 "Your assessment results have been saved and a summary email has been sent to [user's email address]."
UFC-2 The confirmation must be visually distinct (e.g., a green checkmark icon and highlighted box) and appear near the Send Summary to Email button or at the top of the page.
UFC-3 The confirmation must remain visible until the user navigates away or dismisses it.
UFC-4 If there is an error saving results or sending the email, the user must be notified and given instructions to try again or contact support.

### **4. Data Privacy and Security**

DPS-1 The user’s assessment data and email address must be handled securely and only used for the purpose of saving results and sending the summary email.
DPS-2 The user must be informed that their data will not be shared or used for other purposes.

### **5. Accessibility and Usability**

AAU-1 All steps (submission, confirmation, and email receipt) must be clear and accessible to users of all abilities.
AAU-2 The process must work smoothly on both desktop and mobile devices.
